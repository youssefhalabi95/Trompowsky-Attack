Trompowsky Attack

A practical White opening built around 1.d4 Nf6 2.Bg5.

White's opening strategy: White develops the bishop to g5 before committing the b1-knight. This immediately challenges Black's f6-knight. White can exchange the bishop to influence Black's pawn structure or preserve it and use the resulting pressure to develop actively.

The key is to combine central control, efficient development and king safety. The Trompowsky is not a forced attack: Black has several sound ways to equalise, and White must respond to Black's central counterplay.
